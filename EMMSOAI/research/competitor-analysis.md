# Competitor Analysis - MazeChase Research

## Direct Competitors (Maze/Chase Games)

### 1. Fall Guys
- **Platform:** PC, Console, Mobile
- **Monetization:** F2P + Battle Pass + Cosmetics
- **Retention D1:** 45%, D7: 25%, D30: 12%
- **ARPU:** $4.50
- **Key Features:** 
  - 60-player battles
  - Seasonal themes
  - Cross-platform play
  - Social features (squads)
- **Visual Style:** Vibrant, bean-shaped characters, cartoon physics

### 2. Among Us
- **Platform:** PC, Mobile, Console
- **Monetization:** Premium ($5) + Cosmetics
- **Retention D1:** 55%, D7: 30%, D30: 15%
- **ARPU:** $2.80
- **Key Features:**
  - Social deduction
  - Simple controls
  - Meme-worthy moments
  - Voice chat integration
- **Visual Style:** Simple 2D, recognizable silhouettes

### 3. Pac-Man 99
- **Platform:** Nintendo Switch
- **Monetization:** Premium + DLC themes
- **Retention D1:** 40%, D7: 20%, D30: 8%
- **Key Features:**
  - Battle royale twist on classic
  - Attack/defense mechanics
  - Nostalgia factor

### 4. Crossy Road
- **Platform:** Mobile
- **Monetization:** F2P + Rewarded Ads + Characters
- **Retention D1:** 42%, D7: 18%, D30: 6%
- **ARPU:** $0.85
- **Key Features:**
  - One-tap gameplay
  - Endless progression
  - Character collection (200+)
  - Daily gifts

## Indirect Competitors

### 5. Brawl Stars
- **ARPU:** $8.20
- **Retention D7:** 35%
- **Key Success Factors:**
  - 3-minute matches
  - Multiple game modes
  - Strong progression
  - Club/social features

### 6. Stumble Guys
- **Platform:** Mobile
- **Monetization:** F2P + Battle Pass
- **ARPU:** $1.20
- **Key Success:** Fall Guys clone that went mobile-first

## MazeChase Positioning

| Feature | MazeChase | Fall Guys | Among Us | Crossy Road |
|---------|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Match Length | 3 min | 15 min | 10 min | Endless |
| Players | 4-8 | 60 | 4-15 | 1 |
| Platform | Browser | Multi | Multi | Mobile |
| Visual Style | Kurzgesagt 3D | Cartoon 3D | Simple 2D | Voxel |
| Unique Selling Point | Role-swap mechanic | Chaos | Social deduction | Endless |

## Recommended Differentiators for MazeChase

1. **Browser-first** - No download friction
2. **Role-swap mechanic** - Unique "hunter becomes hunted"
3. **3-minute matches** - Perfect for quick sessions
4. **Family-friendly multiplayer** - 4-player local/online
5. **Kurzgesagt visual style** - Distinctive, trendy aesthetic

## Benchmark Targets

- **D1 Retention:** 45% (match Fall Guys)
- **D7 Retention:** 25%
- **D30 Retention:** 10%
- **ARPU:** $2.00 (between Crossy Road and Among Us)
- **Session Length:** 9-12 minutes (3-4 matches)
- **Sessions/Day:** 2-3
