# Monetization Patterns

*Ethical F2P monetization for casual games*

## Revenue Model Overview

### Primary Revenue Streams (Priority Order)
1. **Rewarded Ads** - 60-70% of revenue
2. **Cosmetic IAP** - 20-30% of revenue
3. **Battle Pass** - 10-20% of revenue (optional)

## Rewarded Video Ads

### Best Placements
| Trigger | Offer | Conversion |
|---------|-------|------------|
| After death | Continue with ad | 30-50% |
| Level complete | 2x coins | 40-60% |
| Daily bonus | Bonus reward | 60-80% |
| Shop preview | Try skin for free | 20-40% |

### Guidelines
- Max 1 ad per 90 seconds
- Always make ad watching **optional**
- Clear value proposition before prompt
- Never interrupt gameplay mid-action

### Revenue Benchmarks
- eCPM (US): $15-40 for rewarded video
- eCPM (WW): $5-15 average
- Daily ads per DAU target: 2-4

## In-App Purchases

### Pricing Strategy
| Tier | Price | Content |
|------|-------|---------|
| Micro | $0.99-1.99 | Single skin/item |
| Starter | $4.99 | Bundle + small gems |
| Value | $9.99 | Best value bundle |
| Premium | $19.99 | Large collection |

### What to Sell
✅ Cosmetics (skins, trails, effects)
✅ Convenience (ad removal, auto-collect)
✅ Time-savers (skip timers)
✅ Bundles with perceived value

❌ Pay-to-win upgrades
❌ Essential gameplay features
❌ Competitive advantages

## Battle Pass Design

### Structure
- **Duration:** 4-6 weeks
- **Levels:** 30-50 tiers
- **Free track:** 40% of rewards
- **Premium track:** $4.99-9.99

### Reward Distribution
```
Tier 1-10: Quick wins, keep engagement
Tier 11-25: Core rewards, maintain grind
Tier 26-40: Premium exclusives
Final tier: Ultra-rare exclusive
```

## Player Segmentation

### Spending Categories
| Segment | % of Players | % of Revenue |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Non-payers | 95-98% | 0% |
| Minnows (<$5) | 1-3% | 10-20% |
| Dolphins ($5-50) | 0.5-1% | 30-40% |
| Whales ($50+) | 0.1-0.5% | 40-60% |

### Targeting by Segment
- **Non-payers:** Rewarded ads, soft conversions
- **Minnows:** Starter packs, limited offers
- **Dolphins:** Battle pass, value bundles
- **Whales:** Exclusive content, collections

## Ethical Guidelines

1. **No dark patterns** - Clear pricing, no hidden costs
2. **No FOMO abuse** - Reasonable time limits
3. **No pay-to-win** - Skill should always matter
4. **Clear odds** - If RNG exists, show percentages
5. **Spending limits** - Consider daily/monthly caps
6. **Age gates** - Extra friction for minors

## Key Metrics

- **ARPU:** $0.03-0.10/day target
- **ARPPU:** $3-10/month target
- **Conversion rate:** 2-5% target
- **LTV:** $1-5 for casual games
