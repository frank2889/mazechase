# Market Data & User Metrics Research

## Browser Gaming Market 2024-2025

### Market Size
- **Browser Games Market:** $15.2 billion (2024)
- **CAGR:** 8.2% (2024-2029)
- **Casual/Arcade Segment:** 38% of market

### Platform Distribution
- **Desktop:** 62%
- **Mobile browsers:** 28%
- **Tablet:** 10%

### Key Demographics

#### Age Distribution (Casual Browser Games)
| Age Group | % Players | Avg Session | Spending |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| 13-17 | 15% | 25 min | $2/mo |
| 18-24 | 28% | 35 min | $8/mo |
| 25-34 | 32% | 22 min | $12/mo |
| 35-44 | 16% | 18 min | $10/mo |
| 45+ | 9% | 15 min | $5/mo |

#### Gender Split
- **Male:** 48%
- **Female:** 52%
- **Note:** Casual/party games skew more balanced than core games

### User Behavior Patterns

#### Session Timing
- **Peak Hours:** 
  - 12:00-14:00 (lunch break)
  - 19:00-22:00 (evening)
  - Weekend: 10:00-23:00 (distributed)

#### Device Preferences
- **Chrome:** 65%
- **Safari:** 20%
- **Firefox:** 8%
- **Edge:** 7%

### Monetization Benchmarks

#### ARPDAU (Average Revenue Per Daily Active User)
- **Casual Browser:** $0.05-0.15
- **Midcore Browser:** $0.15-0.40
- **With IAP + Ads:** $0.20-0.50

#### Conversion Rates
- **F2P to Payer:** 2-5%
- **Battle Pass Purchase:** 8-15% of DAU
- **Cosmetic Purchase:** 3-8% of DAU

### Trending Features 2025

1. **Social Integration**
   - Squad/party system: +40% retention
   - Friend challenges: +25% virality
   - Spectator mode: +15% session length

2. **Progression Systems**
   - Daily challenges: +35% D7 retention
   - Season passes: +50% ARPU
   - Unlockable content: +20% session length

3. **Technical Expectations**
   - Load time <3 seconds
   - 60 FPS minimum
   - Cross-save with account
   - Mobile-responsive
